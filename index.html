PROFESSIONAL DISCOVERY

My conversations with professionals

Three short reflections on what I learned from professionals working in aerospace and the space industry.

Airbus · Embedded systems

Pascal — Engineering across a career

Pascal’s career at Airbus showed me that an engineer can move from technical design to system architecture, project management and innovation. His work on flight controls and system safety helped me understand how important reliability and certification are in aeronautics.

What I take from it: I should keep building strong technical skills while staying open to different roles and learning how to work with a team.

Thales Alenia Space · Cybersecurity

Cryptographic equipment for satellites

This professional explained how satellite communication equipment is specified, developed and tested. I learned that cybersecurity in the space industry also relies on electronics, rigorous testing and close coordination between teams. His move from testing to design showed me that a career can develop step by step.

What I take from it: I’m interested in the link between computer science, security and complex industrial systems, where testing and quality are essential.

Space & defence · Configuration management

Amirouche — Keeping complex projects consistent

Amirouche’s role is to make sure technical information stays consistent throughout a product’s life cycle and that every change is properly tracked and validated. I discovered a less visible but important engineering role, connecting design offices, production, maintenance and clients.

What I take from it: A successful engineer needs more than technical knowledge: organisation, communication and teamwork also matter. I also learned that career paths can take unexpected directions.
